## Reading the results

- If the top results are all one page type, a different type rarely wins: match the type, then beat it on usefulness.
- A local pack on a query means local intent: hand to `seo-local` if installed.
- Forums and discussions ranking high suggests people want first-hand experience; plan to include it, honestly.
- Government or regulator pages at the top of a health, money or legal query mean high trust is needed: plan an expert reviewer.
- If the query mixes intents (some guides, some product lists), note both and decide which one the organisation can serve.
